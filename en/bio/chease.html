Founder of Waikiki

Chease Young

Founder of Waikiki, creator of the modern sovereign monarchy, and Chancellor. From state-building reforms on the ruins of an economic collapse to fusion power, the Oceanic Charter, and presiding over the World Government: a journey defined by disciplined strategic stewardship and mentoring rising generations.

👑 King and Founder of Waikiki | 🏛️ Chancellor and Former Dictator | 🏅 Nobel Laureate in Economics (2009) | 🎓 Stanford University (1991) | 🌐 First President of the World Government (2018-2022) | 💍 Husband to Jessica Walker

New York Roots and the Stanford Years 📚

Born in New York in 1972, the pulse of Manhattan and Wall Street taught me the fundamentals of capital cycles and institutional structure from an early age. Growing up within my father's hospitality and property enterprise, I discovered early that my true passion lay not in transient commercial gain, but in large-scale social architecture and sovereign statecraft. Admitted to Stanford University in California in 1987, I immersed myself in macroeconomic theory and quantitative analysis. Graduating with honors in economics in 1991, my academic formation provided the analytical discipline that would later underpin every facet of our state-building mission.

Starlight Hotels and the Foundational Covenant with Jessica 💍

After completing my studies, I assumed leadership of my family's hospitality firm, transforming Starlight Hotels into an internationally acclaimed luxury group. The turning point in my life occurred in early 1992 at the Starlight Hotel in New York, where I met Jessica Walker. Coming from Boston with a distinguished family heritage of acting and public service, Jessica was completing her finance studies at Columbia University. An immediate intellectual bond and mutual respect grew between us, forming an enduring life partnership that would anchor our destiny. Jessica joined the firm as chief financial officer, and in 1993 we married on a private Pacific estate. Our study journey through Singapore and Tokyo provided profound inspiration, demonstrating how disciplined governance and private dynamism can unite in service of the common welfare.

1999: The Birth of a Sovereign Nation from Ruins 🏛️

In 1998, I first visited the Caribbean leading an investment delegation, witnessing firsthand the catastrophic institutional and economic collapse of the Cuban state. When the old regime fell in early 1999, creating a precarious power vacuum, I resolved that passive aid would not suffice: historical responsibility demanded bold executive leadership. On March 10, 1999, I assumed control of the territory and proclaimed the sovereign State of Waikiki. Enacting a disciplined transitional dictatorship, we eliminated the black market, established impeccable public security, and enforced zero tolerance toward corruption. The nation's immense natural wealth—natural gas, crude reserves, and strategic rare earths—was immediately channeled into citizen welfare and institutional modernisation.

Papal Coronation and Building the Meritocratic Welfare Model 👑

Our national constitution, approved by referendum in 2000, established transparent legal order, and our rapid economic ascent validated the reform agenda. Following the resolution of the Pacific conflict, our citizenry resolved through a nationwide vote to anchor executive permanence in a meritocratic royal house. On August 4, 2005, Pope Benedict XVI solemnly crowned me King and Jessica Queen in Nova Aurelia's central square. As monarch, I have always held that sovereignty is validated not by bloodline, but by daily dedication and the tangible prosperity delivered to our citizens. In that spirit, we developed commercial fusion energy, the deep-sea undersea maglev corridor, and established the world's first fully cashless financial infrastructure.

The Nobel Prize and Counter-Cyclical Crisis Governance 🏅

When the 2008 global financial meltdown shattered international banking systems, Waikiki stood entirely resilient. In preceding years, Jessica and I had engineered a counter-cyclical monetary framework backed by sovereign wealth liquidity reserves that insulated our domestic economy from speculative contagions. Rather than burdening taxpayers with banking bailouts, the National Wealth Fund deployed strategic capital to acquire undervalued assets across international markets. In recognition of this groundbreaking macroeconomic stability framework, the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded Queen Jessica and me the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences in 2009, demonstrating that visionary governance transcends geographic size.

Generational Transition: The Chancellor's Mandate and Family Council 🤝

The hallmark of genuine statesmanship is knowing when to entrust executive authority to the next generation. On July 7, 2017, after seventeen years of continuous and intensive operational rule, I stepped down as Dictator, transferring supreme executive governance to our son, Crown Prince Raimondo. Concurrently, our daughters Angelina and Jennifer, alongside their spouses, assumed key leadership in the Senate, creating our synchronized tripartite governance structure. Continuing my service as Chancellor, I oversee strategic foreign policy, constitutional coherence, and mentor the Dynastic Council, proud to watch our children lead with fresh vigor while preserving our founding covenant.

The Oceanic Charter, the World Government, and Quiet Diplomacy 🌐

Throughout my chancellorship, regional stability and global peace have remained my highest priorities. In February 2018, upon my initiative, twelve island nations ratified the Oceanic Partnership Charter, establishing collective environmental and economic security across our maritime region. Later that year, I was honored to be elected as the first President of the World Government, a mandate I held until 2022, facilitating high-stakes mediation and advancing multilateral climate accords. Nova Aurelia has evolved into a premier diplomatic capital: hosting summits on Korean reconciliation and transatlantic commerce, proving that neutrality and intellectual moral authority are the most durable instruments of geopolitics.

Personal Calling, the Sea, and Horizons Ahead ⛵

Whenever official duties permit, I find solace on the open ocean surrounded by my family. Sailing through the Caribbean archipelagos clears the mind of the transient clamor of daily politics. My personal pursuits include numismatics and classical architectural blueprints, alongside teaching an annual masterclass on sovereign macroeconomics at Waikiki Economics University. The ultimate fulfillment of my life is witnessing our grandchildren grow up in a nation defined by pristine natural biomes, technological abundance, and unwavering security—a nation that thirty years ago existed only in our shared imagination.

No state or fortune exists for its own sake: true authority is measured not by accumulated capital, but by the security guaranteed to our citizens, the enduring bond of family unity, and our stewardship for generations to come. 🌴
