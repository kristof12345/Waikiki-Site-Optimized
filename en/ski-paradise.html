Waikiki Ski Paradise

Spanning 1,250 kilometers of velvet-groomed pistes across over 500 slopes, Waikiki Ski Paradise is the largest and most opulent winter sports sanctuary on Earth. Engineered across the snow-crowned High Andes of Brazilia Province and the cryo-cooled summits of Haiti Island, it is the only place in the world where guests carve champagne powder at 4,850 meters in the morning and sail across 28°C Caribbean waters by afternoon.

  1. Sub-Surface Cryo-Grid 3.0

    Over 4,200 kilometers of closed-loop titanium cooling conduits beneath the Haiti Island and lower Andean slopes maintain a constant -4°C snowpack base, preventing slush and preserving crystalline powder even under tropical sunshine.

  2. Pininfarina Heated Maglev Gondolas

    All 180 aerial lifts glide on frictionless magnetic bearings at up to 14 m/s. Every cabin features heated stitched-cashmere reclined seating, electrochromic panoramic glass, champagne chillers and active wind-stabilization gyroscopes.

  3. 18-Minute Sea-to-Summit Vacuum Funicular

    Descending through a pressurized basalt bore from Haiti Glacier Crown (3,180 m) directly to Coral Bay Marina (0 m), the twin-tube funicular accelerates smoothly to 320 km/h with active cabin pressure equalization.

  4. 14,000 Mineral-Crystal Snow Atomizers

    Fed by purified high-altitude glacial meltwater and deep aquifer springs, acoustic snow lances nucleate hexagonal dendritic snow crystals without chemical additives, replenishing 15 cm of fresh velvet powder nightly.

  5. Autonomous Hydrogen Grooming Armada

    A synchronized fleet of 240 zero-emission hydrogen-electric snowcats guided by millimeter-wave LiDAR grooms all 1,250 km of pistes between midnight and 06:00, sculpting corduroy ribs to 0.5-millimeter precision.

  6. Active LiDAR and Acoustic Avalanche Shield

    Quantum weather radar domes and automated micro-impulse acoustic towers continuously monitor snowpack shear layers across every ridge, neutralizing unstable cornices long before morning lift opening.

Beyond the pistes

Sea-to-Summit Lifestyle and Experiences

Designed for sovereign guests who refuse to choose between alpine exhilaration and tropical indulgence, Waikiki Ski Paradise curates experiences found nowhere else on the planet.

Morning Powder, Afternoon Surf

Carve velvet corduroy on Haiti Glacier Crown at 09:00, board the 18-minute vacuum funicular at noon and step directly onto your superyacht or a 28°C coral-sand beach in Coral Bay for afternoon surfing and diving.

Royal Squadron Heli-Skiing

Lift off from Condor Glacier Heliport aboard whisper-quiet hybrid tiltrotors piloted by the Royal Alpine Wing to drop into 40,000 hectares of untouched 4,800-meter Andean bowls with personal mountain guides.

14-Michelin-Starred Summit Dining

Revolving glass rotunda restaurants at 4,000+ meters serve white truffle fondue, Amazonian-Andean tasting menus, vintage Dom Pérignon and Caribbean lobster flown up fresh every morning.

Subterranean Volcanic Thermal Grottos

Glide from the final run straight into naturally heated 40°C mineral lagoons, emerald steam caverns, cryo-recovery chambers and panoramic Finnish cedar saunas overlooking the glaciers.

Aurora-Spectrum Night Skiing

Every evening until 23:00, 320 kilometers of pistes transform into an illuminated theatre lit by low-glare aurora-spectrum LED arrays, accompanied by live symphony projections on the glacier face.

Waikiki One Bespoke Valet

Your custom-fitted carbon skis and heated boots are laser-scanned on arrival and delivered automatically to whichever summit or island locker you select each morning via subterranean pneumatic tubes.

Included with the Sovereign Alpine Privileges Pass

All guests staying at registered Ski Paradise palaces, chalets and observatories receive complimentary unlimited lift access across both the High Andes and Haiti Island, priority seats on the 18-minute Sea-to-Summit Funicular and Trans-Andean Maglev, plus automatic equipment transfer between islands.
