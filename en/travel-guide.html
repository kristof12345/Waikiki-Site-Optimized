Before you go

  1. Check your entry route

    Citizens of American Union, NATO and partner states such as Japan travel visa-free for up to 90 days. Everyone else applies for an e-Visa through the Waikiki One portal.

  2. Passport & insurance

    Your passport must be valid for six months after arrival. Visitors need travel health insurance; state healthcare is free for citizens only.

  3. Plan provincial trips

    Rainforest expeditions in Amazonia require a yellow fever vaccination and a licensed guide; protected reserves admit a limited number of visitors per day.

  4. Download Waikiki One

    The visitor mode stores your e-Visa, transit passes, museum tickets and payments in one secure app.

Getting there & getting around

Waikiki stretches from the Caribbean to the Amazon, yet a modern network keeps every province within easy reach.

By air

Nova Aurelia International is the main gateway, with direct flights from every continent. São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Manaus serve the mainland provinces.

By rail from Florida

The Florida–Waikiki undersea corridor brings maglev services from the United States directly to the islands.

By sea

Cruise terminals in Nova Aurelia and Port Royal welcome ocean liners and private yachts throughout the year.

On the ground

Metro, trams & buses

Urban public transport is free in every major city. Metro lines, electric trams and autonomous buses run around the clock in Nova Aurelia.

Hyperloop

Reach Mega Pyramid City from central Nova Aurelia in minutes on the world's first public Hyperloop line.

Cable cars & elevated rail

An elevated railway serves Palm Island; cable cars and bridges link the islands of the World Islands archipelago.

Water taxis

Electric water taxis connect marinas, island resorts and the waterfront, the only way to reach some of the World Islands.

When to visit

The Caribbean dry season from December to April brings cloudless skies and the year's great festivals. Amazonia is most accessible in the drier months, while the Andes ski season peaks from June to August.

Etiquette & practical tips

Palace & state venues

Smart dress is expected at the Ducal Palace, the Royal Opera and state receptions. Shoulders covered, no beachwear.

Photography

Drones and photography are restricted around the government quarter, military sites and the space centre.

Protect the Blue Belt

Reef-safe sunscreen is mandatory in marine reserves; touching coral and feeding fish are prohibited.

Safety

Waikiki is one of the safest destinations in the world. Emergency services respond through the Waikiki One app in every language.

Tipping

Service is included in hotel and restaurant bills; rounding up for exceptional service is appreciated.

Power & connectivity

Free high-speed Wi-Fi covers public spaces nationwide, a right guaranteed by referendum. Sockets are US-standard.
