Prosperity · Infrastructure

The arteries of the most advanced nation

From undersea maglev corridors and the world's first public Hyperloop to fusion power, artificial archipelagos and an orbital space station, Waikiki builds the infrastructure of the next century today, financed by the National Wealth Fund.

Four pillars of national infrastructure

The Ministry of Infrastructure directs the development and maintenance of roads, the railway network, airports, undersea tunnels and port capacity. Strategic projects are approved by the Senate, which decides on the budget of priority investments and monitors their delivery.

Mobility

Maglev and Hyperloop corridors, an undersea link to Florida, and free urban transport made up of metro lines, electric trams and autonomous buses.

Energy

More than ninety percent of supply comes from solar, wind, nuclear and fusion sources, distributed through one of the world's most advanced electrical grids.

Digital

Internet access is a constitutional right confirmed by referendum. Waikiki One brings every public service onto a single, secure citizen platform.

Ports and Logistics

The fully digitalised commercial port of Nova Aurelia, Port Royal's deep-water terminals and smart port logistics in Recife link Waikiki to world trade.

Corridors that unite the provinces

Waikiki's constitution forbids any restriction on the movement of people or goods between provinces. The transport network turns that principle into concrete, steel and magnetic levitation.

  1. Florida-Waikiki Undersea Corridor

    Initiated by Raimondo Chease, the corridor connects the United States with Waikiki's islands. In phase one, a high-speed rail line was built on the Miami-Nova Aurelia section; in phase two, an undersea highway will be completed along the same route.

  2. The world's first public Hyperloop

    Construction began on the outskirts of the capital in Mega Pyramid City. Capable of speeds of several hundred kilometers per hour, the Hyperloop line connects the megastructure to downtown Nova Aurelia and the business district in minutes.

  3. Free urban transport

    Approved by a large majority in a national referendum, the metro network, electric trams and autonomous buses are available to citizens free of charge across every major city. In Waikiki public transport provides a reliable and convenient alternative to private vehicles.

  4. Island links

    An elevated railway serves Palm Island, while bridges and cable cars connect the 130 islands of the World Islands archipelago along its 200 km coastline. Chameleon Island is primarily accessible by ferries and water taxis that transport tourists and locals between the islands.

An energy system built for abundance

Under the Patriotic Capitalism doctrine Waikiki harnesses the sun, the wind, the atom and oil alike. Revenues from controlled fossil extraction fund the transition, while fusion plants and vast solar arrays now carry the national load.

Hydrogen fusion since 2017

Waikiki commissioned its first commercial hydrogen fusion power plant in 2017. Today, state programmes operate multiple commercial fusion facilities providing vast clean energy.

Hydroelectric power

Harnessing the immense river basins of Amazonia and Brazilia, massive hydroelectric stations complement the grid with steady baseload renewable power.

The most advanced grid

An intelligent national grid, backed by 120 hybrid solar and wind Powerpack stations, balances supply across three provinces and two time zones.

Clean Future 2035

Every new public building in Waikiki since 2025 must carry solar panels, and the national target is a fully carbon-neutral energy supply by 2035.

Mega Buildings

Announced by Raimondo Chease in 2016, the Mega Buildings programme redefines the nation's skyline across two continents. From vertical city-states to monumental pyramid and tower complexes, these engineering marvels integrate residential communities, lush sky-parks, and renewable power into self-contained ecosystems. Together, they establish a new global benchmark for sustainable 21st-century urbanism.

Milestones of construction

Over two decades of national construction, from the capital's intercontinental airport and underground metro to artificial archipelagos and orbital stations.

  1. 2003

    Nova Aurelia Airport

    The nation's intercontinental aviation gateway opens, connecting Waikiki directly to global destinations.

  2. 2006

    Space station core module

    The national space agency places the station's first orbital module in space.

  3. 2007

    Metro lines

    Nova Aurelia inaugurates its first automated underground rapid transit lines, linking key government and residential districts.

  4. 2009

    Palm Island

    Waikiki's first artificial archipelago opens, linked by elevated rail.

  5. 2011

    Sky Paradise on Haiti island

    A panoramic luxury resort and mountain leisure complex opens across the scenic highlands of Haiti.

  6. 2012

    Sky City

    The 956-metre icon completes in Nova Aurelia, standing as the world's tallest skyscraper with vertical gardens and self-sustaining energy.

  7. 2014

    World Islands

    The 130-island archipelago shaped like Earth's continents completes off Nova Aurelia, connected by bridges and cable cars.

  8. 2016

    Disneyland Rio

    The grand entertainment complex opens in Rio de Janeiro, expanding Waikiki's world-class tourism network.

  9. 2016

    Digital port

    Angelina and Taylor oversee the digitalisation of Nova Aurelia's commercial port.

  10. 2017

    First hydrogen fusion plant

    Commercial nuclear fusion joins the national grid, revolutionising clean power generation.

  11. 2018

    Hyperloop network

    Groundbreaking on the first vacuum tube express corridor linking Mega Pyramid City to regional hubs.

  12. 2019

    Mega Pyramid and Royal Clock Tower

    Two icons of the skyline open for the 20th anniversary.

  13. 2019

    Nova Roma project

    Inauguration of the ancient Roman heritage reconstruction complex, resurrecting classical wonders with modern engineering.

  14. 2026

    Chameleon Islands

    The third archipelago completes, combining marine labs, experimental homes and themed hotels.
